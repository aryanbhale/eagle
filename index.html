Eagle Infra wins ₹246 crore stations finishing contract for Mumbai Metro Line 4

Mumbai, India (Metro Rail Today):

In a significant development for the Mumbai Metro Line-4 project, Eagle Infra India has secured the position of the lowest bidder for the architectural finishing and pre-engineered building (PEB) roof contract CA-194. This contract encompasses work on seven stations along the 32.32 km stretch from Wadala to Kasarvadavali.

Eagle Infra India Limited JV wins ₹2,081.57 contract for augmentation of Ambernath water supply scheme in Maharashtra

Mumbai, India (Indian Infrastructure):

The joint venture (JV) of Inderdeep Construction Company and Eagle Infra India Limited has won a contract worth Rs 2,081.59 million for augmentation of the Ambernath water supply scheme under the Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT) 2.0 in Maharashtra. Bids for the same were invited by the Maharashtra Jeevan Pradhikaran (MJP) in September 2024.

EIIL wins ₹809.82 contract for augmentation of existing underground sewerage network in Yavatmal, Maharashtra

Mumbai, India (Indian Infrastructure):

Eagle Infra India Limited has won a contract worth Rs 809.82 million for the augmentation of Yavatmal water supply scheme under the Maharashtra Suvarna Jayanti Nagrothhan Mahabhiyan. Bids for the same were invited by the Maharashtra Jeevan Pradhikaran in March 2024. The scope of work includes providing, lowering, laying, jointing and testing of 19.59 km of distribution system and all other related infrastructure. The work is likely to be completed in 18 months.

Eagle Infra wins contract for development of UGSS at Zone 3 in Nallasopara, Maharashtra

Mumbai, India (Indian Infrastructure):

Eagle Infra India Limited has won a contract worth Rs 6,589.48 million for development of underground sewerage scheme (UGSS) at Zone 3 at Nallasopara in the Vasai Virar Municipal Corporation (VVMC) area under the Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT) 2.0. Bids for the same were invited by the VVMC in January 2024. The scope of work includes construction of a sewer collection system, sewage pumping station, sewage pumping main, sewage treatment plant having a capacity of 51.5 million litres per day (mld) with supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) automation.
